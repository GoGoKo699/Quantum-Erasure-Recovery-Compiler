# Relation to existing recovery methods

## Measurement-only extraction

Morley-Short, Gimeno-Segovia, Rudolph and Cable's stabilizer pathfinding finds logical Pauli representatives that anticommute on the output and commute locally at every other site. Their heralded-loss patterns use only available qubits. That condition is the zero-CNOT case of the [cost theorem](theorem.md). Bell, Pettersson and Paesani use this type of criterion to optimize graph codes for measurement-based loss tolerance and to study teleportation, measurement and fusion in the photonic setting. The paid-edge problem asks what recovery costs after the measurement-only criterion fails, with the surviving hardware graph included in the input. [Morley-Short et al., arXiv:1807.08729](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.08729) · [Bell et al., PRX Quantum 4, 020328 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.020328)

Shiraishi, Yamasaki and Murao give a polynomial-time one-way LOCC extraction algorithm for a logical qubit in an intact stabilizer encoding when the desired output is connected to the reference in the graph-state representation. They also study how cooperation among subsets of parties affects access. Their method is a baseline for the pure-state and zero-paid-interaction cases. The state after tracing an erased subset is generally a mixed stabilizer state, and the recovery circuit here can use a finite budget of specified CNOT couplers. Shi, Patil and Guha give single-qubit measurement decoding constructions, including planar-code logical strings that intersect at exactly the output. Those strings supply a zero-cost pair in the present model whenever they avoid the erased locations. [Shiraishi et al., Phys. Rev. A 110, 052617 (2024)](https://link.aps.org/accepted/10.1103/PhysRevA.110.052617) · [Shi et al., PRX Quantum 6, 010339 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.6.010339)

## Unlocking mixed stabilizer states

Smolin showed that a four-party state can be unlocked by a joint Bell measurement at two of the sites. For the generalized Smolin family, Wang and Ying write the state as the maximally mixed stabilizer subspace specified by the all-$X$ and all-$Z$ correlators, up to eigenvalue conventions. They explain why joint measurements within suitable blocks unlock entanglement and describe the pairwise Bell measurements for the even-qubit family. The [examples](benchmarks.md) realize such states after a correctable erasure. The cost theorem works for arbitrary surviving stabilizer groups, optimizes over all eligible correlator pairs, assigns a cost to each hardware edge, and supplies optimal native gates with a matching adaptive converse. [Smolin, Phys. Rev. A 63, 032306 (2001)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0001001) · [Augusiak and Horodecki, Phys. Rev. A 73, 012318 (2006)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0411142) · [Wang and Ying, Phys. Rev. A 75, 052332 (2007)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0703033)

## Classical decoding and circuit synthesis

Maximum-likelihood erasure decoding usually selects a likely Pauli coset given flagged locations and a syndrome. For example, Delfosse and Zémor give a linear-time algorithm for surface codes. Such a decoder normally outputs a correction description for an encoded block. The task here ends with an unknown quantum state on a single usable qubit. Erasure correctability and a classical Pauli correction do not specify the CNOT cost of this physical extraction. [Delfosse and Zémor, Phys. Rev. Research 2, 033042 (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.01517)

Circuit-synthesis and graph-transformation results address other parts of the design space. Kumabe, Mori and Yoshimura characterize two-qubit gate complexity for graph-state preparation and transformation by elementary edge complementations. Dahlberg, Helsen and Wehner show hardness for extracting a prescribed collection of Bell pairs from a graph state by local operations and classical communication. The [complexity result](complexity.md) here concerns one Bell pair with an inaccessible reference, a possibly mixed stabilizer input, and a variable budget of paid two-qubit gates. [Kumabe et al., Quantum 10, 2165 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2026-07-18-2165) · [Dahlberg et al., Quantum 4, 348 (2020)](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2020-10-22-348)

Matsumoto minimizes the number of stabilizer measurements needed for syndrome-based erasure correction. A multi-site stabilizer measurement is a primitive in that accounting. The objective here counts CNOTs on specific couplers and allows individual Pauli measurements and resets without charge. [Matsumoto, arXiv:2510.22890](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.22890)
